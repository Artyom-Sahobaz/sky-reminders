Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

In short: Sky Reminders reads your calendar so it can show reminders on your computer. Your calendar data stays on your computer. We do not have servers that receive it, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone.

Who we are

Sky Reminders is a desktop app made by Flow Virtual Assistant (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at myassistant@flowvirtualassistant.com.

What the app accesses

When you choose Continue with Google or Continue with Microsoft, you give the app permission to read:

DataWhy
Your email addressTo show which account is connected.
Your calendar events for the next 36 hours: title, start and end time, location, description, meeting link, organizer and guest namesTo show a reminder before each meeting and the details and join link when you click it.

Access is read-only. The app cannot create, change or delete events, and it does not read your email, contacts or files.

Where your data is kept

  • The app talks directly to Google or Microsoft from your computer. Your calendar data is not sent to us or to any server of ours.
  • The sign-in token that lets the app keep reading your calendar is stored on your computer, encrypted with your Windows user account.
  • Calendar events are held in the app's memory while it runs. The app keeps a small local list of which reminders it has already shown, for up to 3 days.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising, and the app does not send usage data anywhere.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Google user data

Sky Reminders' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove your data

  • In the app, open Settings and click Remove next to an account. Its sign-in token is deleted from your computer.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
  • You can also cancel the app's access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Microsoft at account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or myapps.microsoft.com (work accounts).

Children

Sky Reminders is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.
