Sky Reminders by FlowVA

Never miss a meeting again.

Sky Reminders by FlowVA is a free Windows app. Before each meeting on your Google Calendar, a little plane or hot-air balloon flies onto your screen. Click it to see the details and join in one click.

Download for Windows
Free · Windows 10 and 11 · Read-only calendar access

How it works

1. Connect

Click Continue with Google and sign in with your Gmail or Google Workspace account. That's all the setup.

2. Get a heads-up

15 minutes before a meeting a plane flies in. At 5 minutes, a balloon floats up.

3. Join

Click it for the time, guests and notes, then Join. Double-click to snooze for 2 minutes.

What Sky Reminders does with your calendar

  • It reads your upcoming events (title, time, location, notes, meeting link, guests) to show reminders on your computer.
  • It never changes your calendar, and it doesn't read your email, contacts or files.
  • Your calendar data stays on your computer. It is not sent to us, sold or shared.
